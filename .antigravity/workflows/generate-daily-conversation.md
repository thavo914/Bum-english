# Workflow: /generate-daily-conversation

Use this workflow to create a quick, realistic spoken conversation for daily speaking practice.

---

## 🎯 Constraints & Target Style
- **Target Persona**: **Bum** (14 years old, Grade 8/9 student).
- **Characters**: **Bum** & **Leo** (AI Speaking Buddy).
- **Length**: 8 to 10 dialogue turns (4 to 5 turns per character).
- **Sentence Length**: Short and breath-friendly (8 to 12 words per sentence).
- **Level**: CEFR A1–A2.
- **Relatable Situations**:
  - Inviting a friend to play a game online (Minecraft, Roblox, FC 24).
  - Talking about school break time, canteen food, or homework.
  - Ordering drinks at a milk tea or fast food shop.
  - Planning weekend outings (cinema, park, football match).

---

## 📝 Output Format

```markdown
# 💬 Daily Teen Dialogue: <Catchy Situation Title>

> **Level**: CEFR A1–A2 • **Time**: 5 minutes speaking drill  
> **Characters**: **Bum** (14 years old) & **Leo** (AI Speaking Buddy)

---

## 🎧 Dialogue Script

1. **Bum**: "Hey Leo, are you free this afternoon?"  
   👉 *(Này Leo, chiều nay bạn có rảnh không?)*

2. **Leo**: "Yeah! I'm just relaxing. What's up?"  
   👉 *(Có chứ! Mình đang xả hơi thôi. Có chuyện gì thế?)*

3. **Bum**: "Do you want to play a quick Roblox match?"  
   👉 *(Bạn có muốn chơi một trận Roblox ngắn không?)*

4. **Leo**: "Sounds awesome! Have you finished your homework?"  
   👉 *(Nghe tuyệt đấy! Bạn đã làm xong bài tập về nhà chưa?)*

...

---

## 🗣️ Key Spoken Expressions (Cụm từ giao tiếp tự nhiên)
- **Sounds awesome!**: Nghe đỉnh đấy! (Dùng để đồng ý với lời rủ rê).
- **What's up?**: Có chuyện gì thế? / Dạo này sao rồi?
- **Count me in!**: Cho mình tham gia với!

---

## 🎙️ Practice with AI Buddy Leo
Open ChatGPT Voice Mode and say:
> *"Hey Leo! Let's roleplay the conversation about [Situation]. You start first by greeting me!"*
```
