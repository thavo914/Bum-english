## 2. Grammar Bite: *Have got / Has got* (Sở hữu)

In casual British and international English, people frequently use **have got** / **has got** to say what they possess.

### The Rule Formula:
- **I / You / We / They** + **have got** (Short form: *I've got / You've got*)
  - *I've got a wireless mouse.*
- **He / She / It** + **has got** (Short form: *He's got / She's got*)
  - *He's got a big curved screen.*

### Negatives & Questions:
- **Negative**: *haven't got* / *hasn't got*
  - *I haven't got a webcam.*
  - *She hasn't got a new phone.*
- **Question**: *Have you got...?* / *Has he got...?*
  - *Have you got a gaming headset?* -> *Yes, I have! / No, I haven't.*

> 💡 **Watch out**: *He's got* = *He has got*. (It does NOT mean *He is* here!)
