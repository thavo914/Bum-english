## 2. Grammar Bite: *Can / Can't* for Abilities

We use **can** to express what we are able to do, and **can't** (cannot) for what we cannot do.

### The Rule Formula:
- **Subject** + **can** + **Verb (base form)**
  - *I can build a fortress in Fortnite.*
  - *He can run at high speed.*
- **Subject** + **can't** + **Verb (base form)**
  - *I can't beat this level alone.*
  - *They can't speak Japanese.*

### Questions:
- **Can you + Verb...?**
  - *Can you jump over the lava?* -> *Yes, I can!* / *No, I can't.*

> 💡 **Super Easy Rule**: **Can** never changes! No *-s*, no *-ed*, no *-ing*.  
> *He can plays* ❌ -> *He can play* ✅
