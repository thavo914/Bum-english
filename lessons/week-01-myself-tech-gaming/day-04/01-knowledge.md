# Day 4: Daily Routine & Stream Time ⏰

Today we look at how to talk about your daily schedule, after-school habits, and gaming time using the **Present Simple**!

---

## 1. Word Power (5 Words for Today)

| Word | Pronunciation (IPA) | Vietnamese Meaning | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **routine** | /ruːˈtiːn/ | thói quen hàng ngày, lịch trình | *My evening **routine** starts after homework.* |
| **usually** | /ˈjuː.ʒu.ə.li/ | thường thường (khoảng 80% lúc) | *I **usually** do my homework at 7 PM.* |

---

## 2. Grammar Bite: Present Simple (Thì Hiện Tại Đơn)

We use the **Present Simple** for things we do regularly, habits, and daily routines.

### The Rule Formula:
- **I / You / We / They** + **Verb (raw)**
  - *I **play** games after school.*
  - *We **watch** YouTube in the evening.*
- **He / She / It** + **Verb + s / es**
  - *He **plays** football on Saturdays.*
  - *She **watches** anime.*

### Frequency Words (Always, Usually, Sometimes, Never):
Put them **BEFORE** the main verb:
- *I **usually** finish my homework early.*
- *He **sometimes** plays online games.*

### Negatives & Questions:
- *I **don't** play games late at night.*
- *He **doesn't** stream during the week.*
- *Do you play Minecraft?* -> *Yes, I do! / No, I don't.*

## 3. Mini-Dialogue: After-School Chat 🎒

- **Ken**: "What do you usually do after school?"
- **Nam**: "I **usually** do my homework first, then I **relax** and watch a live **stream**."
- **Ken**: "Do you play games on weekdays?"
- **Nam**: "Only for 45 minutes! My parents say one **match** per day is enough."
