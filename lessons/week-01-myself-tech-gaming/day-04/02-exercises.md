## Part 1: Routine Vocabulary Fill-in

Fill in the blanks with: `[ routine | usually | relax | stream | match ]`

1. After studying hard for 2 hours, I need 15 minutes to ____________.
2. What is your morning ____________ before going to school?
3. We won the first game, but we lost the second ____________.
4. He wants to ____________ his Roblox adventures this weekend.
5. I ____________ ride my bicycle to school with my friends.

## Part 2: Correct Verb Form

Choose the correct form of the verb in brackets:

1. He (play / plays) Valorant with his friends every weekend.
2. I (usually study / study usually) in my bedroom.
3. They (don't / doesn't) go to bed late on school days.
4. She (watch / watches) gaming tips on YouTube every afternoon.
5. (Do / Does) you have dinner with your family at 7 PM?
