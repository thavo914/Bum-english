## 2. Grammar Bite: Verb *To Be* (am / is / are)

We use the verb **To Be** to say who we are, our age, and how things are.

### The Rule Formula:
- **I** + **am** (*I am 14 years old.* / Short form: *I'm 14.*)
- **He / She / It** + **is** (*He is a great gamer.* / Short form: *He's a gamer.*)
- **You / We / They** + **are** (*We are online friends.* / Short form: *We're online friends.*)

### Negatives & Questions:
- **Negative**: Add **not** (*I am not a pro player.* / *He isn't online.*)
- **Question**: Put To Be first (*Are you ready?* -> *Yes, I am!* / *Is he your teammate?* -> *No, he isn't.*)

> 💡 **Pro-Tip**: Don't say *"I have 14 years old."* In English, always say: **"I am 14 years old."**
