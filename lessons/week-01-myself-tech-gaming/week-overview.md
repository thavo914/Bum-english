# ⚡ Week 1: Myself, Tech & Gaming
> **Target Audience**: 14-year-old student (Grade 8 / 9)  
> **Level**: CEFR A1–A2  
> **Goal**: Master personal introductions, talking about tech gear, basic abilities, daily gaming habits, and chatting with AI Buddy Leo.

---

## 🗓️ Daily Schedule

| Day | Theme | Target Grammar | Core Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1** | **My Gamer Profile & Tech Setup** | Verb *To Be* (`am`, `is`, `are`) | Name, age, favorite game, gamer tag |
| **Day 2** | **Gaming Gear & Gadgets** | *Have got / Has got* | Headsets, keyboards, screens, mice |
| **Day 3** | **Superpowers & Skills** | *Can / Can't* for abilities | In-game skills, speed, teamwork |
| **Day 4** | **Daily Routine & Stream Time** | *Present Simple* | Daily schedule, streaming, relaxing |
| **Day 5** | **Weekly Boss Battle & Leo Chat** | Review & Speaking Practice | 10-minute voice chat mission with Leo |
