## 2. Grammar Bite: Week 2 Grammar Master Recap

Let's quickly review the 4 powers you built in Week 2:

1. **Possessive Adjectives**: *My friend, his avatar, her book, our squad.*
2. **Like/Love + V-ing**: *I like playing soccer.* / *He loves watching movies.*
3. **Some vs Any**: *I want some pizza.* / *Have you got any bubble tea?*
4. **Present Continuous**: *I am chatting now.* / *What are you doing?*
