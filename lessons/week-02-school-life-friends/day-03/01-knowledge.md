## 2. Grammar Bite: *Some* vs *Any*

We use **some** and **any** to talk about quantities when we don't need to specify the exact number.

### 1. Countable vs Uncountable:
- **Countable** (can count: 1, 2, 3...): *apples, hot dogs, snacks, coins*
- **Uncountable** (cannot count individually): *water, milk, cheese, money, ice*

### 2. The Golden Rule of Some & Any:
- Use **SOME** in positive statements:
  - *I want **some** snacks.*
  - *There is **some** bubble tea in the fridge.*
- Use **ANY** in negative statements and questions:
  - *I haven't got **any** money.*
  - *Are there **any** seats in the canteen?*
  - *Have you got **any** pizza left?*

> 💡 **Polite Request Exception**: When offering or asking politely for food, use **some**:
> - *"Can I have **some** water, please?"* ✅
> - *"Would you like **some** fries?"* ✅
