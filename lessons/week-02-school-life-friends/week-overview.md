# 🎒 Week 2: School Life, Friends & Fun
> **Target Audience**: 14-year-old student (Grade 8 / 9)  
> **Level**: CEFR A1–A2  
> **Goal**: Describe best friends and classmates, discuss favorite subjects and hobbies, order food and snacks, talk about what you are doing right now, and roleplay a weekend hangout.

---

## 🗓️ Daily Schedule

| Day | Theme | Target Grammar | Core Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1** | **My Squad & Best Friends** | Possessive Adjectives (*my, your, his, her, our, their*) | Classmates, best friend, traits |
| **Day 2** | **School Subjects & Hobbies** | *Like / Love / Hate + V-ing* | Science, PE, projects, preferences |
| **Day 3** | **Canteen Snacks & Bubble Tea** | *Some* vs *Any* (Countable / Uncountable) | Ordering snacks, food, drinks |
| **Day 4** | **What Are You Doing Right Now?** | *Present Continuous* (*am/is/are + V-ing*) | Hanging out, studying, chatting |
| **Day 5** | **Weekend Hangout & Leo's Cafe** | Review & Speaking Roleplay | Ordering at a bubble tea shop with Leo |
