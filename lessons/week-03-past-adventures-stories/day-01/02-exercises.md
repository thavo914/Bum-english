## Part 3: Sentence Transformation & Mini-Translation (Viết Lại Câu & Dịch) ✏️

## Part 4: Thử Thách Phục Thù (Rematch Challenge - Lỗi ERR-06) 🥊

Yesterday afternoon, the blue squad played remarkably well and ________ the championship match.
- A. win
- B. won

- D. is winning
