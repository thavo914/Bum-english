# Repository Agent Guidelines: A-Level English Engine for 14-Year-Old Nephew

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Core Directive**: All lessons, practice drills, and AI interactions in this repository are designed specifically for **Bum**, a **14-year-old student (Grade 8 / 9, Secondary School / THCS)** learning English at the **CEFR A1–A2 level** (Elementary / Pre-intermediate).

---

## 1. Pedagogical & Linguistic Principles

### 1. Teen-Relatable & High Engagement (No Baby Talk, No Corporate Jargon)
- **Target Persona**: **Bum**, a 14-year-old teenager with authentic hobbies: video games (Minecraft, Roblox, mobile gaming), tech gadgets, football and sports, school life & friends, YouTube/music, fast food, and movies/anime.
- **Strictly Avoid**:
  - Kindergarten/toddler baby talk (*"the cute little teddy bear"*).
  - Adult corporate clichés (*"synergy"*, *"bring to the table"*, *"exceed expectations"*).
  - Heavy academic grammatical terminology.

### 2. The 2-File Daily Architecture
Every daily lesson belongs to a dedicated folder: `lessons/week-<N>/day-<M>/` consisting of **exactly 2 files**:
1. **`01-knowledge.md` (Definitions & Core Input)**:
   - **Word Power**: 5 target words with IPA phonetics, clear Vietnamese meanings, and visual example sentences.
   - **Grammar Bite**: 1 core A1-A2 pattern with a simple slot-filling formula, examples, and common traps.
   - **Mini-Dialogue**: A lively 4-line teen chat putting the words & grammar into context.
2. **`02-exercises.md` (Interactive Practice & Drills)**:
   - **Part 1: Quick Check** (Multiple choice / word match).
   - **Part 2: Sentence Builder** (Fill-in-the-blanks / word unscramble).
   - **Part 3: Mini-Translation** (Vietnamese to English, practical teen situations).
   - **Part 4: AI Voice Mission with Leo** (Prompt for ChatGPT Voice / Gemini Live Buddy).
   - **Answer Key** (Collapsible `<details><summary>Click to reveal answers 🔍</summary>`).
   - **Scorecard** (3-Star self-evaluation ⭐ ⭐ ⭐).

---

## 2. Git & Version Control Rules
- **Do Not Auto-Commit or Auto-Push**: Keep all files in the local working tree. Never commit or push without explicit instructions from the user.
